Elemental Balls: credits

Concept art: Aarib (Void Crystal, Frost and Toxic Current).

The balls' looks are the concept pictures projected onto the ball by unreal/Scripts/project_concepts.py. The materials,
the glass shell mesh and the model files are made by the scripts in unreal/Scripts/ (Unreal Engine 5.8.3, Python with
numpy, scipy and Pillow). No third-party assets are included.

models/sphere.glb is this plugin's own 1 m sphere.
